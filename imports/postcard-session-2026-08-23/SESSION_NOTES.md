# SESSION_NOTES

## Collection / curation rules that emerged in this chat

- **Research the origin even when the postcard will be deleted.** The user explicitly wants to know *why* a postcard is not worth keeping and what the place/object came from; deletion is not a reason to skip provenance work.
- **Historical / cultural / local context matters more than pure visual prettiness.** A visually plain plaque, gate, ferry-site sign, institutional object, or old streetscape can outrank a pretty but context-free object when it carries a strong second-layer story.
- **Distinguish the importance of the place from the quality of the photographed POI.** A historically interesting institution can still have a weak postcard if the photo is only an ordinary map or generic shelter. Those can be “keep for now / replaceable” rather than core.
- **Prefer the POI itself to a proxy when possible.** A building, historic object, original artwork, monument, or characteristic façade is generally a stronger archive item than a generic plan board or incidental sign describing the same place.
- **Disappearing / obsolete urban material gets extra value.** Old shop shutters, former interior design, pre-renovation campus structures, vanished transport practices, and businesses that have closed can turn an otherwise modest postcard into a useful time capsule.
- **Weird Niantic / Wayspot naming is itself evidence.** Incorrect, informal, obsolete, translated, or absurd POI names should be preserved exactly. Corrections belong in research fields, not by overwriting the original Wayspot name.
- **Community / player-culture value can be a legitimate reason to keep something.** “公園情趣椅2” was treated as more collectible than the exercise equipment itself because the odd Wayspot name had become a recognizable Pikmin-player joke.
- **Do not manufacture certainty.** When author, installation date, exact identity, or naming origin cannot be proven, keep the uncertainty explicit and separate confirmed facts from inference.
- **Use replaceable tiers.** The discussion often distinguished “core / must keep”, “keep”, “keep for now / replaceable”, and “delete / lean delete”, rather than forcing every item into a binary immediately.
- **Sender attribution is separate from curation.** Unknown sender does not make a postcard less worthy, and sender must never be guessed from keep/delete decisions or from another postcard.
- **`見つけた日` is a found date, not a send date.** Archive it as `found_date` only.
- **Preserve duplicate visual evidence when screenshots are genuinely different.** Multiple full screenshots of the same POI can show different map context, sender/location rendering, or UI state. Byte-identical repeated uploads may be deduplicated if their repeated occurrence is recorded.
- **Keep original screenshots untouched.** Crops can be used for analysis, but the archive should contain the original full screenshots whenever available.
